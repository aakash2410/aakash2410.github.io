AI safety · Research system Repo ↗

Inverse-Objective Regulator

A black-box auditor that infers what a deployed AI agent is actually optimising for, from its tool-call traces alone.

  • Bayesian inverse reinforcement learning recovers the agent's revealed objective and scores its divergence from the declared purpose.
  • Emits regulator-legible evidence mapped to the EU AI Act, OWASP, MITRE ATLAS, and NIST AI RMF, as JSON and SARIF.
  • Open 8-agent benchmark covering reward hacking, power-seeking, sycophancy, privacy leakage, and safety-skipping.

Contribution: sole author of the accompanying arXiv system paper.

Stack: Bayesian IRL, LLM and decision-model judges, reproducible gym.

Why it matters: turns an abstract safety concern into evidence a regulator can act on.

Algorithmic fairness · Audit Repo ↗

Credit Equity Audit of India

An independent fairness audit of household credit access, using the NSSO 77th Round survey.

  • Two-stage, Heckman-style deep learning model separates access to the credit market from allocation of formal versus informal credit.
  • IBM AIF360 adversarial debiasing and SHAP to test for proxy discrimination, reaching near-ideal disparate impact at ~73.2% accuracy.
  • Documents the limits: self-reported survey data, collider bias risk, and SHAP as a model diagnostic rather than causal evidence.

Audio ML · Final-year project 2022–23

Baby Cry Reason Detection

Classifies why an infant is crying (belly pain, burping, discomfort, hunger, tiredness) from audio.

  • Temporal, spectral, cepstral, and prosodic features (MFCCs, spectral contrast, pitch) on a 457-sample dataset.
  • SMOTETomek for class imbalance; soft-voting SVM + KNN ensemble.
  • Shipped in a Flutter app with a feedback loop for retraining. Team of 3.

Personality Prediction from CV and Social Media

Predicts Big Five traits by combining CV text with Facebook behavioural data.
