Research and Independent Work

Inverse-Objective Regulatorgithub.com/aakash2410/Inverse-Objective-Regulator
  • Black-box auditing system that infers a deployed agent's revealed objective from its tool-call traces via Bayesian inverse reinforcement learning, scores divergence from its declared purpose, and emits regulator-legible evidence mapped to the EU AI Act, OWASP, MITRE ATLAS and NIST AI RMF.
  • Open 8-agent benchmark of agentic failure modes (reward hacking, power-seeking, sycophancy, privacy leakage, safety-skipping). A deterministic open cross-encoder critic ranks the planted objective top-2 on every seed for 6 of 8 agents (mean r = 0.55), deterministically and with no API cost, against a frontier LLM-as-judge spanning 0.08–0.43 across agents. Sole author of the paper draft (not yet on arXiv) and the implementation.
Credit Equity Audit of India (NSSO 77th Round)github.com/aakash2410/CreditAudit-India
  • Two-stage, Heckman-style framework separating access to the credit market from allocation of formal versus informal credit. Female-headed households had ~59% of the probability of market entry of male-headed households (disparate impact 0.59), against the conventional 0.8 threshold.
  • IBM AIF360 adversarial debiasing and SHAP to test for proxy discrimination, lifting disparate impact from 0.59 to 0.94 at ~73.2% accuracy. Documented the design's limits: self-reported survey data, collider bias risk, and SHAP as a model diagnostic rather than causal evidence.
The Specialist, on the Suno Sutra edge platformgithub.com/aakash2410/SpecialistAISunoSutra
  • Grounded offline retrieval with explicit abstention on the open-source AI device from Digital India Bhashini (MeitY) and Current AI, which runs vision, ASR, translation and TTS on-device across 22 Indic languages. Answers are grounded in approved DIKSHA content and carry a citation to chapter, section and page.
  • Two gates enforce abstention: retrieval refuses below a similarity threshold before an LLM call is spent, and the grounded prompt makes the model emit a no-answer signal the pipeline converts into a refusal. multilingual-e5-small embeddings with a deterministic TF-IDF fallback keep the loop reproducible without a neural model.
  • Designed the device's inference pipeline and owned the benchmarking behind it: batching and scheduling tradeoffs across three concurrent models under a hard RAM ceiling cut latency to first audio from ~8s to 2–3s and peak memory by 40%.
rag-compliance-auditorgithub.com/aakash2410/MCP_RAG_Compliance
  • Sole-authored package on PyPI. MCP server that audits RAG and AI pipelines against the EU AI Act, GDPR/DPDP, HIPAA, SEBI/RBI and NIST AI RMF across 7 audit dimensions; RS256-signed audit reports, 8 MCP tools, CI gate with SARIF reporting.
