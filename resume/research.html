Research and Independent Work

Inverse-Objective Regulatorgithub.com/aakash2410/Inverse-Objective-Regulator

Black-box AI-safety auditing system; sole author of the accompanying arXiv paper.

  • Infers a deployed agent's revealed objective from its tool-call traces via Bayesian inverse reinforcement learning, scores divergence from its declared purpose, and emits regulator-legible evidence mapped to the EU AI Act, OWASP, MITRE ATLAS and NIST AI RMF.
  • Released an open 8-agent benchmark spanning agentic safety failure modes (reward hacking, power-seeking, sycophancy, privacy leakage, safety-skipping). A deterministic open cross-encoder critic recovers planted objective divergence on 6 of 8 agents (mean r = 0.55), beating a frontier LLM-as-judge (mean r 0.08 → 0.43) with near-zero variance and no API cost.
  • Full Python implementation: Bayesian IRL, LLM and decision-model judges, a reproducible gym, and JSON/SARIF reporting.
Credit Equity Audit of India (NSSO 77th Round)github.com/aakash2410/CreditAudit-India

Independent fairness audit of household credit access and allocation.

  • Two-stage, Heckman-style deep learning framework separating access to the credit market from the allocation of formal versus informal credit among households already in it.
  • Female-headed households had about 59% of the probability of entering the credit market compared with male-headed households (disparate impact 0.59), against the conventional 0.8 threshold.
  • Applied IBM AIF360 adversarial debiasing and SHAP to test for proxy discrimination, reaching near-ideal disparate impact at ~73.2% accuracy. Documented the limits of the design: self-reported survey data, collider bias risk, and SHAP as a diagnostic of the model rather than causal evidence.
The Specialist, on the Suno Sutra edge platformgithub.com/aakash2410/SpecialistAISunoSutra

Grounded offline retrieval with explicit abstention, on a government-backed edge device.

  • Built and packaged a subject-expert module for Suno Sutra, the open-source offline AI device from Digital India Bhashini (MeitY) and Current AI, which runs vision, ASR, translation and TTS fully on-device across 22 Indic languages.
  • Answers are grounded in approved DIKSHA content and carry a citation to chapter, section and page. Two gates enforce abstention: retrieval refuses below a similarity threshold before an LLM call is spent, and the grounded prompt makes the model emit a no-answer signal the pipeline converts into a refusal.
  • multilingual-e5-small embeddings with a deterministic, dependency-free TF-IDF fallback fitted at build time, so the loop remains reproducible without a neural model and the refusal threshold stays backend-aware.
  • Harvesting of DIKSHA assets is gated pending bulk-download and offline-redistribution licensing.
